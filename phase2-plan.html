SECTION 02
Advanced Order Tracking

Customers today expect to see exactly where their order is, in real-time. This module introduces WebSocket-powered live tracking, a full location history trail, and an estimated arrival engine.

Live Driver Location

Delivery drivers broadcast their GPS coordinates every few seconds via Pusher. Customers and admins see a live-updating map pin without refreshing the page.

ETA Engine

A server-side calculation combines current driver coordinates, distance to destination, average speed, and traffic heuristics to provide a live Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA).

Location History Trail

Every driver ping is stored in a driver_locations table. Admins can replay the full route of any completed delivery — useful for dispute resolution.

Order Status Timeline

Each status change (placed → accepted → preparing → picked up → delivered) is timestamped and visible to the customer as an interactive step-progress view.

SECTION 03
Advanced Push Notifications

Phase 1 introduced basic FCM push notifications. Phase 2 evolves this into a full notification intelligence layer — segmented, scheduled, personalized, and multi-channel (FCM + In-App + SMS), with rich media support and deep-link routing.

Segmented Campaigns

Admins can send push notifications to targeted user segments: all users, specific cities, users who haven't ordered in 7 days, top spenders, or users of a specific module.

Scheduled Notifications

Admins schedule notifications for a future datetime. A background job queue (Laravel Scheduler) processes and dispatches them at the right time in the right timezone.

Rich Media & Deep Links

Notifications support image attachments, action buttons ("Track Order", "View Offer"), and deep links that open specific screens in the mobile app directly.

Notification Centre

All sent notifications are stored and retrievable by users as a notification inbox — read/unread state, timestamps, and direct links to the relevant content.

Delivery Analytics

Track open rates, click-through rates, and conversions per campaign. Admins see a dashboard showing which notifications drove the most orders.

Automated Triggers

Event-driven notifications: "Your order is ready!", "Driver is 5 minutes away!", "Rate your last order", "You have a new voucher!" — all sent automatically based on system events.

SECTION 04
Advanced Platform Features
A — AI-Powered Product Recommendations

Leverage order history to surface the right products to the right users at the right time. A lightweight recommendation engine analyzes each customer's purchase patterns and returns personalized product lists on the home screen, increasing average order value.

"Order Again" Section

Returns frequently-ordered items for the current user, sorted by recency and frequency.

Trending Products

Aggregates top-selling items across a rolling 7-day window, segmented by module or city.

"You May Also Like"

Collaborative filtering: users who ordered product A also ordered product B — served as add-on suggestions at checkout.

Personalized Home Feed

Home ads and offer banners are ranked based on the user's category affinity score.

B — In-App Chat (Customer ↔ Support / Driver)

A real-time chat layer powered by Pusher allows customers to communicate directly with their delivery driver or with support staff without leaving the app — eliminating the need for external messaging apps.

  • Customers can message their driver once an order is accepted
  • Admins have a support inbox to handle escalated customer queries
  • Messages are stored per-order for full conversation history
  • Unread message badge count synced in real-time via Pusher channels
  • Chat closes automatically once order is marked delivered
C — Advanced Analytics & Business Intelligence Dashboard

Extend the existing admin dashboard with granular, actionable metrics and visual reports that help management make data-driven decisions.

Revenue Heatmap

Hour-by-hour and day-by-day revenue heatmap showing peak ordering periods.

Customer Lifetime Value

CLV score per customer: total spend, order frequency, churn risk indicator.

Funnel Drop-off Report

Tracks how many users browse → add to cart → checkout → complete. Shows abandonment rate at each step.

Driver Efficiency Score

Composite score from delivery time accuracy, customer ratings, and completion rate.

SECTION 04 (CONT.)
D — Loyalty & Rewards System

Increase customer retention by rewarding repeat purchases. Users earn points for every order, which can be redeemed for discounts or free delivery — creating a powerful habit loop.

Points Engine

Configurable earn rate (e.g. 10 pts per EGP 50). Points are credited on order delivery.

Redemption at Checkout

Users can apply points as a discount at checkout. Min/max redemption rules are admin-configurable.

Tier System

Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum tiers unlock better earn rates, priority support, and exclusive offers.

Points Ledger

Full history of earned and spent points per user. Admins can manually adjust balances and issue bonus points.

SECTION 04 (CONT.)
E — Smart Search & Discovery

Replace simple keyword matching with a full-text, fuzzy, and context-aware search experience across products, stores, categories, and offers — with auto-complete and recent search history.

  • Fuzzy search: handles typos ("burgr" → "burger") using Laravel Scout + Meilisearch
  • Searchable entities: Products, Stores, Categories, Modules, Offers
  • Auto-complete suggestions appear after 2 characters typed
  • Search results ranked by relevance score + user preference affinity
  • Recent searches stored per user; popular searches shown as trending chips
