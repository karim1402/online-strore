makook Sprint 1 — SMS OTP & Advanced Push Notifications Page 2
Sprint 1 Communication & UX Enhancement
📱
FEATURE 1.1
SMS OTP Authentication

Replace or supplement email-based verification with a one-time password sent via SMS. This increases registration conversion and provides a second factor for sensitive actions (login, checkout, password reset).

Registration OTP

New users verify their phone number before account activation. OTP expires in 5 minutes. Up to 3 resend attempts allowed.

Login via Phone

Users can log in using phone number + SMS OTP as an alternative to email/password — ideal for less tech-savvy customers.

Password Reset via SMS

A secure OTP is sent to the user's registered number to authorize a password reset without needing email access.

Provider & Fallback

Primary: Vonage (Nexmo) or Twilio. Configurable via .env. Automatic fallback to email OTP if SMS fails.

🔔
FEATURE 1.2
Advanced Smart Push Notifications

Go beyond basic order alerts. Introduce intelligent, event-driven notifications that target users based on their real-time cart and browsing behavior — turning passive users into active buyers.

Cart + Offer Trigger

When an admin adds a new offer/discount on a product that is currently sitting in a user's cart, that user instantly receives a push: "🔥 Good news! [Product] in your cart is now on sale — grab it before it sells out!"

Cart Abandonment Nudge

If a user hasn't checked out after 2 hours with items in cart, an automated push is fired: "You left something behind! Complete your order now."

Back-in-Stock Alert

Users who viewed or wishlisted an out-of-stock product are notified automatically when it becomes available again.

Price Drop Alert

If a product's price drops after a user has viewed it in the past 48 hours, they receive a personalized price drop notification.

💡 Logic: A scheduled Laravel Job runs every 15 minutes checking: active carts → matching offers → users who haven't received this notification in the last 24h → dispatch FCM via queue.
makook Sprint 1 — Real-Time Chat & Delivery App UI Page 3
💬
FEATURE 1.3
Optimized Real-Time Chat

The current chat is basic. Phase 2 Sprint 1 upgrades it into a fully-featured in-app messaging system between customers and their delivery driver — with message history, typing indicators, and read receipts.

Typing Indicator

Real-time "Driver is typing…" indicator powered by Pusher private channels — improves the feel of responsiveness.

Read Receipts

Messages show a single tick (sent), double tick (delivered), and blue tick (read) — synced in real-time via Pusher.

Quick Reply Templates

Drivers see pre-set quick reply buttons: "On my way!", "I've arrived", "Slight delay" — reduces friction for drivers on the road.

Chat Lock After Delivery

Chat is automatically closed and made read-only once the order status reaches "Delivered". History remains accessible for 7 days.

Media Messages

Customers and drivers can send a single image (e.g. driver sends photo of delivered order, customer sends location screenshot).

Unread Badge Sync

Unread message count is synced in real-time to the app's notification badge — no polling required.

🛵
FEATURE 1.4
Delivery App UI Optimization

The delivery driver's app experience must be fast, clear, and distraction-free. This optimization pass focuses on reducing the steps a driver needs to take to accept, navigate, and complete deliveries.

🗺

One-Tap Navigation

A single button on the order detail screen opens the customer's address directly in Google Maps or Waze.

⚡

Quick Status Update Bar

A persistent bottom bar lets drivers change order status with one tap — no deep navigation required.

📋

Redesigned Order Cards

Order cards show: customer name, distance, payout amount, and item count at a glance — optimized for splitting-second decisions.

🔕

Do Not Disturb Mode

Drivers can pause new order notifications during a short break — automatically re-enables after a set duration.

✅ Sprint 1 Deliverable: Fully working SMS OTP flow · Cart-aware push notifications · Upgraded chat with typing indicators & read receipts · Delivery app with one-tap navigation and redesigned order interface.
makook Sprint 2 — Loyalty & Rewards System Page 4
Sprint 2 Loyalty & Rewards System
🎁
FEATURE 2.1
Points-Based Loyalty System

Reward customers for every order they complete. Points accumulate in a personal wallet and can be redeemed as a discount on future orders. The system is simple, transparent, and admin-configurable — no tier levels.

Points Earning

Customers earn points for every completed order. Example: 10 points per EGP 50 spent. Admin can configure the earn rate from the dashboard.

Points Wallet

Each user has a points balance visible in their profile. The wallet shows current balance, all earned transactions, and all redemptions.

Redeem at Checkout

Users can apply points as a checkout discount (e.g. 100 pts = EGP 5 off). A minimum redemption threshold is set by admin (e.g. 200 pts min).

Points Expiry

Points expire after a configurable period of inactivity (e.g. 6 months without an order) to encourage continued use.

🏆
FEATURE 2.2
Milestone Gift Vouchers

Automatically reward users when they hit meaningful milestones — creating a "surprise & delight" moment that builds emotional loyalty and directly incentivizes more orders.

🎉

5-Order Milestone

When a user completes their 5th order, they automatically receive a gift voucher (e.g. 20% off or EGP 25 free) — delivered via push notification and email.

💰

EGP 5,000 Spend Milestone

When a user's cumulative spend reaches EGP 5,000, they receive a special voucher — rewarding high-value customers with a meaningful benefit.

🔧 How it works: A Laravel Observer listens on the orders table for status changes to delivered. It recalculates the user's total order count and total spend. If a milestone threshold is crossed (and hasn't been awarded before), it generates a unique voucher code and dispatches a push notification + email.
📊
FEATURE 2.3
Admin Loyalty Dashboard

Points Overview

Total points issued, redeemed, and outstanding. Cost of liability shown in EGP equivalent.

Milestone Report

How many users hit each milestone. Voucher redemption rate. Revenue attributed to loyalty-rewarded orders.

Manual Points Adjustment

Admins can add or deduct points from any user account with a mandatory reason note — useful for customer service resolutions.

Configurable Milestones

Admin can add, edit, or remove milestone rules (e.g. change 5 orders to 10, or add a 10,000 EGP milestone) without code changes.

✅ Sprint 2 Deliverable: Points earning & redemption at checkout · Points wallet in user profile · Automated milestone gift vouchers (5 orders & EGP 5,000 spend) · Admin loyalty management dashboard.
makook Sprint 3 — Smart Search, AI Recommendations & Pro Subscription Page 5
Sprint 3 Intelligence & Growth Engine
🔍
FEATURE 3.1
Smart Product Search

Replace simple LIKE queries with a full-text, fuzzy, typo-tolerant search powered by Laravel Scout + Meilisearch — delivering instant, relevant results across products, stores, and categories.

Fuzzy & Typo-Tolerant

"burgr" → "burger", "piza" → "pizza". Handles common Arabic/English transliteration variations.

Auto-Complete

Suggestions appear after 2 characters. Results update in real-time as the user types — powered by a debounced API call.

Recent & Trending

Each user's last 10 searches are stored locally. A global trending list shows the top 10 most-searched terms in the last 24h.

Multi-Entity Search

A single query searches across Products, Stores, Categories, and active Offers — results are grouped by entity type.

🤖
FEATURE 3.2
AI-Powered Product Recommendations

Leverage order history and browsing data to serve the right products to the right user at the right moment — increasing average order value and session engagement.

"Order Again" Section

The home screen shows the user's top 5 most-ordered products sorted by recency — one-tap reorder.

"You May Also Like"

At checkout: "Customers who ordered this also added…" — collaborative filtering based on historical co-purchase patterns.

Personalized Home Feed

Categories and featured products on the home screen are reordered based on the user's purchase history affinity score.

Trending Near You

Shows the top-selling products in the last 7 days based on the user's current city or selected delivery area.

⭐
FEATURE 3.3

A recurring monthly/annual subscription that unlocks premium benefits for power users — creating a reliable recurring revenue stream and increasing retention.

⭐ Pro Benefits

  • Free delivery on every order
  • Exclusive member-only offers
  • Priority support queue

  • 2× loyalty points earn rate
